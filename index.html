Opportunity brief

How I'd approach Tech CapEx during the integration

MISUMI has announced a global investment plan of about ¥150 billion, and Fictiv's platform is built on AI-driven workflows. Development work like that is where technology capitalization decisions sit. A clean, consistent tracker makes the JHQ numbers easier to defend.

Questions I'd ask first

  1. Which development projects are being capitalized today, and under which policy?
  2. What evidence of stage and hours does the auditor expect?
  3. How does JHQ want capitalized spend and its OpEx impact reported?

How I'd work on it

  1. List every open project with spend, stage and owner.
  2. Tie the tracker to the ledger each month.
  3. Show the OpEx that follows, so the forecast reflects it.
